---
name: Support tickets and Inbox
description: User-defined boundary between handover records and conversation handling.
---

Support tickets should show the customer, the reason for human handover, and an Open conversation link. All replies and conversation handling belong in the Inbox, not a duplicate ticket composer.

**Why:** The user said the entire purpose of Support tickets is to show that a chosen conversation was given to a human/team member rather than handled by AI, and that repeating the message container does not make sense.

**How to apply:** Keep tickets as an escalation index. Preserve assignment-required suggested replies by exposing their editing and sending within the Inbox.
